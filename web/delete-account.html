Releaf account control

Delete your Releaf account

You can permanently delete your Releaf account through our secure browser flow. You do not need the Android app installed.

For security, Releaf asks you to sign in before an account can be permanently deleted. The deletion action is performed for the authenticated account only.
  1. Open the secure Releaf account page using the button below.
  2. Sign in with the email address and password for the account you want to delete.
  3. Select Delete account.
  4. Confirm Delete permanently.
Continue to secure account deletion

If you cannot sign in, use Forgot password? on the secure account page first. After successful deletion, the Releaf account and account-associated server data handled by the account service are deleted. Data that exists only on a user's device is not remotely accessible to Releaf and must be managed through that device's app/storage controls.
