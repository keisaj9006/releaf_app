## Production acceptance still required

- Mindfulness Basics is the only owner-approved recorded meditation. File existence is checked for export integrity; it is not voice approval, licensing evidence or a loudness/decoding measurement.
- Each unrecorded session needs an approved voice render, measured clip duration and silence within its unchanged step interval, caption-to-recording review, mix listening and session-specific visual/content acceptance. The approved 0.82x voice contract stays blocked until its identity is recovered; do not substitute another narrator.
- screenGuidance is concise on-screen guidance, not a verbatim transcript of spokenGuidance. Exported start/end values describe programmed step boundaries, not measured speech offsets.
- Existing previews, captions-only defaults, independent ambience/voice controls, bundled playback and pause/resume have automated coverage. Automated coverage does not establish owner content approval or reliable long-duration hardware playback.
- Both unguided timers now start silently; their configured Deep Drift is available only after explicit opt-in. Session-local toggles preserve the saved guided-session preference. Both timers and preference restoration have regression tests; Samsung Unguided 5 UI was checked separately from owner listening.
- Provenance/licence records, measured LUFS/dBTP/sample rates, technique evidence and per-card visual approval remain separate production requirements. This table does not infer them from a non-empty asset path. [Basics source measurements](../release/2026-09-12-approved-narration-measurements.md) now cover its four approved recordings and separate ambience; rendered-mix/phone acceptance and other assets remain pending.
