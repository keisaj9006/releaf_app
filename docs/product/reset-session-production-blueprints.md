## Shared production requirements and unverified fields

- Preserve IDs, access/privacy, programme durations, breathing ratios and reward semantics. Emergency remains free and outside ordinary progress.
- Approved recorded Releaf Guide only, baseline 0.82x, no substitute voice or TTS. Missing narration remains silent. Human breathing cues require licensed originals and owner approval; holds/rest have no breathing cue.
- Narration, optional ambience and breathing cues stay separate. Music BPM, acoustic spectrum, measured LUFS/true peak, ducking and delivered voice WPM are not established by metadata; measure actual approved assets.
- Retain silent/no-words, reduced-motion, stop/exit and lifecycle handling. Screen-reader, haptic, headphone and physical comfort review remain pending. Shared implementation is not proof of every card passing hardware QA.
- The rationale below is existing catalogue copy, not a new research conclusion. Per-method evidence strength, limits, sensory comfort, unique visual acceptance and competitive differentiation require further review; no clinical advantage is claimed.
- Programme intervals below are exact scheduled seconds. User-driven guided-step advancement may finish earlier. Captions are not assumed to be measured speech timestamps.
- All per-session provenance/licence records, owner approval, device observations and final content acceptance remain open unless separately evidenced. Do not treat an asset path or a non-empty script as approval.
- Catalogue visual values are metadata, not a claim that every player uses that widget directly. Emergency has a dedicated player/visual override; movement demonstrations also override generic visuals. Empty catalogue fields do not imply that the existing screen has no guidance.
