# Spatial Span 50-level progression — 30 September 2026

Spatial Span now has a playable, bounded level-50 profile. Its live cap alone rises from 12 to 50; Memory, Labyrinth, Sequence Echo and N-back already support 50, while the other 10 registered Brain games remain capped at 12. The existing game ID, route, stored completion count, two-completions-per-level rule, score formula and rewards are unchanged. Earned levels above the old cap appear without data migration.

For levels 1–12, tests pin every difficulty and round's grid side, sequence length, light/gap timing and random seed. Levels 13–50 use a 4×4 or 5×5 board, 8–14 positions per sequence and bounded 260–410 ms light and 100–135 ms gap durations. The sequence generator and lifecycle cancellation remain unchanged. Tests cover every higher level/difficulty/round, distinct challenge-band parameters and a 320 dp level-50 screen that plays the pattern and accepts the first correct touch. Physical-device comfort, touch accuracy and accessibility at higher levels remain open.
