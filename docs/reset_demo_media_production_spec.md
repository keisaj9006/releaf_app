# Releaf Reset — Demo Media Production Specification

## Scope

Demo media is deliberately selective. Releaf does not add video to a Reset
because video is visually attractive; it is used only when seeing the movement
materially reduces ambiguity about how to perform it.

Current approved demo sessions:

- `pushups-activation` — 10 Push-Ups Activation
- `shake-it-out` — Shake It Out

All other current Reset sessions remain text/animation-led unless the product
owner explicitly changes their `ResetDemoRequirement`.

## Production format

- short technique demonstration: 15–40 seconds;
- no narration is required in the demo asset;
- no music is baked into the asset;
- instructions, accessibility text and session audio remain separate app layers;
- avoid fitness-performance framing, intensity claims or outcome promises;
- movement must look controlled, calm and achievable rather than aspirational;
- the video must not autoplay in the Reset hub.

## Push-Ups Activation

The demonstration must show:

1. a controlled standard floor repetition;
2. a wall alternative;
3. a seated press alternative;
4. a clear visual message that the user chooses the comfortable version.

Do not demonstrate speed repetitions, failure, exhaustion or competitive
exercise behaviour.

## Shake It Out

The demonstration must show:

1. small loose movement through hands and arms;
2. optional light leg movement when standing;
3. a seated alternative;
4. a deliberate return to stillness.

Do not use aggressive full-body shaking or imply that stronger movement is more
effective.

## Release gate

A demo asset is not product-ready until:

- the movement matches the current Reset instructions;
- the safety note remains compatible with what is shown;
- both required accessibility alternatives are visible;
- the file is licensed/owned for Releaf;
- QA confirms playback on the target Android device;
- the catalog points to a real bundled asset.

Until then, the existing Reset remains fully usable without a demo and the UI
must not expose a placeholder, disabled video button or “coming soon” state.
