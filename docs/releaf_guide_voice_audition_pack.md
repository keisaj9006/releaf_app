## Core audition prompt

Female British English voice, early 30s to early 40s. Warm low-mid register. Calm, grounded and intimate without whispering. Natural conversational phrasing with soft sentence endings and unforced breaths. Slow pace, but not sleepy unless explicitly directed. Reassuring without sounding therapeutic, parental, performative or like a commercial voiceover. Avoid radio cadence, exaggerated emotion, ASMR breathiness and synthetic sing-song rhythm. The listener should feel that one calm person is sitting nearby and guiding them.

## Audition text

Let your eyes close if that feels comfortable.

Notice the places where your body is already supported by the surface beneath you.

There is nothing to fix right now.

Allow the next breath to arrive on its own.

And when your attention moves somewhere else, notice that gently.

You do not need to force it back.

Just return to one simple sensation, and give yourself a little more time.

## Direction C — Settling / pre-panic
- same identity as A
- lowest urgency
- longer pauses
- softer consonant attack
- no dramatic emotional colouring

## Blind listening scorecard

Score 1–5:
1. Sounds like a real person
2. Calmness
3. Warmth
4. Trust / comfort
5. British accent naturalness
6. No obvious AI cadence
7. Comfortable after repeated listening
8. Clear at low volume
9. Does not sound like an advert/podcast
10. Feels distinctive enough to become Releaf Guide

Any candidate scoring below 4/5 on “sounds like a real person” or “no obvious AI cadence” is rejected regardless of total score.
